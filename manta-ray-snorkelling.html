Manta Ray Snorkelling: Where To Go & What To Expect

Gliding next to a manta ray is pure magic. Here's where to do it in Bali, Komodo and the Maldives, and how to do it right.

Home › Travel Guides › Manta Ray Snorkelling Guide

By Gabs, Founder of That Girl Experiences · Updated November 2026

Quick AnswerThree of the best places to snorkel with manta rays are Nusa Penida (Bali), Komodo National Park and the Maldives. Mantas have no stinger and no teeth, they're completely harmless to people, and you don't need to dive to see them. All three of our women-only trips include manta ray snorkelling with local guides.

Is It Safe To Swim With Manta Rays?

Yes. Unlike stingrays, manta rays have no stinging barb and no teeth for biting. They're filter feeders that eat plankton. They're big (reef mantas can be several metres wide) but gentle and curious. The only rules are about protecting them: don't touch, don't chase, and give them space.

1. Nusa Penida, Bali

Just off Bali, Nusa Penida is home to reef manta rays that visit cleaning stations here throughout the year, which is why sightings are so reliable (our Bali guides see them on around 9 out of 10 trips). Boats leave from neighbouring Nusa Lembongan or Penida itself, and the snorkel spots sit below dramatic limestone cliffs. It's often combined with a day exploring Kelingking Beach.

2. Komodo National Park, Indonesia

Komodo is famous for dragons, but the ocean is the real star. Strong currents bring plankton-rich water that attracts manta rays to sites such as Manta Point, and snorkellers can often watch them from the surface. The best way to reach the top spots is on a boat trip or liveaboard from Labuan Bajo, combining mantas with Pink Beach, Padar Island and Komodo dragons.

Tips For The Best Manta Encounter

  • Float calmly at the surface and let the mantas come to you.
  • Never touch a manta (it damages the protective coating on their skin).
  • Stay out of their path and don't dive down on top of them.
  • Go with operators who don't feed or bait wildlife.
  • Bring a GoPro, but put it down for a minute and just watch. It's unreal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I snorkel with manta rays near Bali?

Nusa Penida, a short boat ride from Bali and Nusa Lembongan. Reef manta rays visit cleaning stations there throughout the year.

Do manta rays sting?

No. Manta rays have no stinging barb and no teeth. They're gentle filter feeders and completely safe to swim with.

Can beginners snorkel with manta rays?

Yes. You don't need any diving experience. Basic swimming ability is recommended and guides are with you in the water.

Are manta sightings guaranteed?

No wildlife encounter can be guaranteed, but Nusa Penida, Komodo and the Maldives all have very high sighting rates with experienced local guides.
