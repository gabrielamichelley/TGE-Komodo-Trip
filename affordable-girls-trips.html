That Girl Experiences runs all three with Small Groups (8-10 women max) and 2 girls max per room.

What Makes A Girls Trip Affordable (Not Just Cheap)?

"Cheap" trips often cut corners: hostels, dorms, 20+ person groups or hidden extras. "Affordable luxe" means you get beautiful stays and bucket-list experiences at a fair price because a group shares the costs. When comparing trips, check:

  • Is accommodation twin-share in hotels or villas, or dorms?
  • How many meals and activities are actually included?
  • Are transfers, boats and entry fees included?
  • How big is the group?
  • Is the deposit low, and is there a clear cancellation policy?

How To Save On Your Girls Trip

  • Book early or in sales. Prices usually rise as departures fill up, so watch for launch and sale offers.
  • Join the VIP list on any trip page for first access to new dates and deals.
  • Come solo and share a room instead of paying a single supplement.
  • Fly smart: book flights only once your trip is confirmed to go ahead, and compare nearby airports.
