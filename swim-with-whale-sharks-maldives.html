How To Swim With Whale Sharks In The Maldives

The gentle giants of South Ari Atoll are one of the world's greatest wildlife encounters, and you don't need to be a diver to meet them.

Home › Travel Guides › Swim With Whale Sharks In The Maldives

By Gabs, Founder of That Girl Experiences · Updated November 2026

Quick AnswerThe best place to swim with whale sharks in the Maldives is South Ari Atoll, home to the South Ari Marine Protected Area, where whale sharks can be found all year round. Stay on Dhigurah local island right next to the protected area, snorkel at the surface with a licensed local guide, keep your distance and never touch them. Our Maldives Girls Retreat includes a guided whale shark snorkel for women travelling solo or with friends.

Why South Ari Atoll Is The Place

Most places in the world only see whale sharks for a few weeks or months a year. South Ari Atoll is different. The South Ari Marine Protected Area (often shortened to SAMPA) is known for a population of whale sharks that can be seen year round, which is why it's on so many bucket lists. The sharks here are mostly juveniles cruising the outer reef, often close to the surface, which makes this one of the very best places on earth to see them while snorkelling.

The island of Dhigurah sits right beside the protected area. Staying here (rather than at a far-away resort) means short boat rides, more time in the water and a much more affordable trip.

When Is The Best Time To See Whale Sharks?

You can see whale sharks in South Ari Atoll in any month. Calmer, clearer seas are typical of the drier months, while the wetter months bring fewer crowds, warm water and lush islands. Either way, it's wildlife, so no operator can honestly guarantee a sighting. Good local guides spot them on the large majority of trips because they know where the sharks like to feed.

Do You Need To Be A Diver Or Strong Swimmer?

No. Whale sharks are usually seen from the surface, so this is a snorkelling experience. You should be comfortable in the water and able to float with a mask, snorkel and fins (life jackets are often available). Your guide briefs you on the boat, then you slip in quietly and swim alongside.

How To Swim With Whale Sharks Ethically

  • Go with a licensed local operator who follows the Maldives whale shark guidelines.
  • Keep a respectful distance, never touch or chase the shark, and never block its path.
  • No flash photography, and don't kick hard near the shark. Calm, slow fins are best.
  • Limit the number of snorkellers in the water at once and keep encounters short.
  • Wear reef-safe sunscreen (or a rash guard) to protect the reef and the sharks.

What To Pack

  • Rash guard or long-sleeve swim top (sun + reef safe)
  • Your own mask if you have one (snorkel gear is usually provided)
  • Waterproof phone pouch or GoPro
  • Seasickness tablets if boats aren't your thing
  • A light cover-up for walking through the village (local island dress code)

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best place to swim with whale sharks in the Maldives?

South Ari Atoll, around the South Ari Marine Protected Area. Dhigurah island sits right beside it, which makes it the most convenient base.

Can you see whale sharks in the Maldives all year?

Yes. South Ari Atoll is known for whale sharks being present year round, although sightings are never 100% guaranteed because they're wild animals.

Is it safe to swim with whale sharks?

Yes. Whale sharks are gentle filter feeders that eat plankton and tiny fish. You snorkel at the surface with a local guide, keep a respectful distance and follow local guidelines.

Do I need scuba certification?

No. Whale shark encounters in South Ari are done snorkelling from the surface. Beginners are welcome if they're comfortable in the water.
