Our Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

That Girl Experiences ("TGE", "we", "us") is a women's small-group travel company based in Australia, providing affordable luxe girls trips, group tours & travel experiences for women worldwide. We take your privacy seriously and only collect what we need to run your trip and answer your questions. This policy explains how we handle your personal information when you visit thatgirlexperiences.com, contact us, join our list or book a trip. Questions? Email us at thatgirlexperiences@gmail.com.

What We Collect

Information You Give Us
When you fill in a form, download a brochure, join the VIP list, take the Travel Identity Quiz, message us or book, you may give us:
  • Your name, email address and phone or WhatsApp number
  • Which trip you're interested in and any questions you ask
  • Booking details such as passport name, date of birth, dietary needs, room preferences and emergency contact (collected during booking, usually through WeTravel)
  • Messages you send us by email, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook or live chat
Information Collected Automatically
When you browse our website we and our service providers may collect technical information such as your IP address, device and browser type, the pages you view, how far you scroll, where you click, how long you stay, and the ad or link that brought you to us (for example the UTM or campaign details in the link you clicked). We use cookies and similar technologies to do this (see below).
Payment Information
Bookings and payments are handled by WeTravel. Your card details are entered into WeTravel's secure checkout. TGE does not see or store your full card number.

How We Use It

What We Do With Your Information
  • To answer your questions and send the brochure or trip details you asked for
  • To process your booking, take payments and organise your trip with our guides, hotels, boats and other suppliers
  • To email or message you about your trip, and (if you've joined our list or enquired) to contact you or tell you about new trips, offers and updates. You can unsubscribe at any time
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it
  • To measure and improve our advertising and show our ads to people who may be interested in our trips
  • To meet our legal, tax and safety obligations
We never sell any of your personal information.

Cookies, Pixels & Analytics

Tools We Use On Our Website
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook & Instagram): records actions such as viewing a trip page, clicking Book Now or submitting a form, so we can measure our ads and show them to people likely to be interested. Meta may combine this with information it already holds about you under its own privacy policy.
  • TikTok Pixel: does the same for our TikTok ads.
  • Microsoft Clarity: records anonymised sessions and heatmaps (clicks, scrolling and mouse movement) so we can see where visitors get stuck. If you submit a form, your name or email may be attached to your session so we can follow up with you. Clarity does not record what you type into payment fields. See Microsoft's privacy statement.
  • Tawk.to live chat: if you start a chat, your messages (anonymous unless you've specifically provided your contact details) are stored by Tawk.to so we can reply.
  • WeTravel: our secure, third-party booking and payment platform, embedded on our pages. WeTravel has its own cookies and privacy policy.
Your Choices
You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use a private window, or install a tracker-blocking extension. The website will still work, though some features (like live chat) may not. To control ads you see from us, use your ad settings on Facebook/Instagram or TikTok, or opt out of Clarity tracking by emailing us.

Who We Share It With

Our Service Providers
We share information only as needed with trusted providers that help us run TGE, including WeTravel (bookings and payments), Formspree (website forms), Sender and similar email tools, ManyChat and WhatsApp/Meta (messaging), Google (email and spreadsheets we use to manage enquiries), Tawk.to, Microsoft, Meta and TikTok (as described above), and the hotels, guides, boat operators and transport companies delivering your trip (for example, giving a hotel your name and dates). Some of these providers are located overseas, including in the United States and Europe, so your information may be processed outside Australia. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

Your Rights

Access, Correction & Deletion
You can ask us at any time to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (unless we need to keep it for legal or accounting reasons). You can unsubscribe from marketing using the link in any email or by telling us. If you are in the UK or EU you also have rights under the GDPR, including to object to or restrict certain uses of your information. Email thatgirlexperiences@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. If you're not happy with our response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).
How Long We Keep It & How We Protect It
We keep enquiry and booking information for as long as it's useful to look after you and to meet our legal and tax obligations, then delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable safeguards, such as password-protected accounts and reputable providers, but no online system is completely secure, so please take care with what you share.
Children
Our trips and website are for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
Changes To This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date it was last changed.
