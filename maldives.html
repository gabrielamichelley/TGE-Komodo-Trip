Why Dhigurah?

The World's #7 Best Beach

Dhigurah is a long, lush local island in South Ari Atoll, famed for its endless beach, its sandbank, and the whale sharks, manta rays and abundant marine life that move through its turquoise waters. This is the Maldives Dream without the resort price tag — crystal clear waters, white-sand beaches, warm Maldivian hospitality, and the kind of marine life most people only see in documentaries.

#7World's 50 Best Beaches 2026

Dhigurah's beach has just been named #7 in THE WORLD'S 50 BEST BEACHES in 2026.

Being the #7 best beach in the world and in the beautiful Maldives, Dhigurah offers unique landscapes, peaceful, untouched surroundings, pristine beaches and a tropical island-feel. Dhigurah has the longest sandbank in the Maldives with the whitest sand, bluest, clearest waters, surrounded by bucket-list marine animals like Whale Sharks all year long.

💙 Why I chose Dhigurah

It's no doubt Maldives has some of the most beautiful beaches in the world and has been a bucket-list destination for so many people - it's a must-visit because it does not disappoint. I fell in love with the Maldives the first time I went - its beauty, white-sand beaches, beautiful crystal-clear waters and diverse marine life truly meets your expectations. I chose Dhigurah and the South Ari Atoll because this local island offers the best that Maldives has to offer, whether you're an ocean-lover, adventurous or simply just want to experience relaxing in The Maldives: Whale Sharks, Manta Rays, Spinner Dolphins, Sea Turtles, Nurse Sharks, Reef Sharks, Tropical Marine Life and Diverse, Colourful Fish you've never seen before, White-Sand Beaches, Turquoise Waters, Coconut Trees, Magical Sunsets & Tropical Vibes - all year long!

The Maldives - made accessible & for solo girlies

We do affordable luxury girls trips like no other. You don't have to wait for anyone to go to the Maldives and we know it's not the easiest or cheapest place for solo travellers! This is why we created the Maldives Girls Trips: more affordable, accessible & social - so you can experience the best of Maldives, live out your island dreams with like-minded girls, still with all the adventures & inclusions at a more affordable price, small groups for a more personalised experience, and safe solo travel for girls, without any of the planning.

🏝️ Dhigurah's Endless Sandbar Escape

Dhigurah Beach stands out for its unusually long stretch of coastline and the sandbar that extends far into the lagoon at the top. It's easily reached from the village, yet quickly feels removed, especially as you continue toward the far end where the sandbar begins. Here, you will find a narrow ribbon of soft white sand that pushes out between shallow, crystal-clear water on both sides. The conditions are typically calm, making it ideal for long walks and amazing swims, while sections of reef nearby are teeming with healthy marine life. It's a true tropical paradise and the scale and shape of the sandbar give Dhigurah a unique element that sets it apart from other Maldivian beaches.

Will I definitely see whale sharks?
Dhigurah sits beside the South Ari Marine Protected Area, home to the only known resident year-round whale shark population in the world, and operators here sight them on the large majority of trips. It's wildlife, so we can never promise a sighting — but this is one of the best places on earth to see them! We're all about seeing these gentle giants ethically and in their natural habitat - with no feeding & no captivity.
Is it safe to swim with whale sharks?
Yes! Whale sharks are gentle filter feeders - they only eat plankton & tiny fish and don't harm humans at all. We snorkel at the surface with a local guide, keep a respectful distance and follow the local whale shark guidelines.
Are there any meals included?
Yes! Most meals on the trip are included (half-board). Daily Breakfast & 2-course Dinners (ala-carté) at the rooftop restaurant are included every day. You can choose a Starter + Main or a Main + Dessert for your dinner. The restaurant has a diverse menu featuring Maldivian, Asian and Western dishes, prioritising locally-sourced, fresh ingredients. Local farmers grow coconut, screwpine, breadfruit and chilli, while seafood dishes highlight the catch of the day. Due to local religious customs, pork and alcoholic drinks cannot be consumed on any local island.
What kind of accommodation will we be staying at?
We'll stay at Seaside Dhigurah, one of Dhigurah's finest 4-star boutique villas, on Dhigurah island in South Ari Atoll. You'll be in a Deluxe Garden Villa (twin-share, 2 girls max per villa) with twin beds, a private wooden deck, outdoor rainfall shower, air-con, free Wi-Fi & a bicycle for every guest. All rooms are twin-share with only 2 girls max per room. No hostels or backpacker dorms ever.
Do I need to be an experienced snorkeller/swimmer?
Not at all! You don't need to be a strong swimmer, but you should be comfortable being in the water. All our water excursions use professional guides, snorkel gear is provided for the whole stay, and life jackets are available on every trip. Beginners are absolutely welcome — you don't need any prior scuba diving or snorkelling experience!
What if I have dietary requirements?
We can accommodate most dietary requirements with advanced notice. If you have any dietary requirements, please let us know in your booking notes so we can accommodate and prepare for it. Due to religious customs in the Maldives, pork and alcoholic drinks cannot be consumed on any local island. We'd love for you to join us and we want to make sure everyone is well taken care of!

Can I drink alcohol in the Maldives?
Dhigurah is a local island, so no alcohol is sold on the island, aside from what's included on our luxury resort day (6 cocktails & 4 mocktails at the resort). It is illegal to consume alcohol in local islands in the Maldives.
What should I wear on a local island?
Dhigurah is a local island. That means a respectful dress code in the village — shoulders and knees covered away from the beach — and a designated bikini beach where bikinis & swimwear is absolutely fine. We'll send you a full packing list before you fly!
