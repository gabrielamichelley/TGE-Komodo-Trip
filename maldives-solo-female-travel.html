The Maldives For Solo Female Travellers

You don't need a honeymoon or a $1,000-a-night resort to see the Maldives. Here's how women are doing it solo, affordably and safely.

Home › Travel Guides › Maldives For Solo Female Travellers

By Gabs, Founder of That Girl Experiences · Updated November 2026

Quick AnswerYes, the Maldives is great for solo female travellers. The secret to an affordable Maldives trip is staying on a local island (like Dhigurah) in a guesthouse or boutique hotel instead of a private resort island, then visiting a luxury resort for the day. Joining a women-only group like our Maldives Girls Retreat also removes the single supplement worry, since you share a twin room with a matched roommate.

Why Most People Think The Maldives Isn't For Solo Travellers

The Maldives is marketed with overwater villas and couples. Resorts often price rooms per villa, so solo travellers pay double, and seaplane transfers add up fast. But since local islands opened up to guesthouse tourism, there's a whole other Maldives: real island villages, beautiful public beaches and the same turquoise water, at a fraction of the price.

Local Island vs Resort: The Difference

  • Cost: local island hotels cost a fraction of resort villas, and speedboats replace seaplanes.
  • Experience: you see real Maldivian life, eat local food and meet locals.
  • Rules: local islands follow Islamic customs, so no alcohol is sold and you dress modestly in the village. Swimwear is fine on the designated bikini beach and on boat trips.
  • Best of both: book a resort day pass for cocktails, infinity pools and white-sand luxury.

Is The Maldives Safe For Women?

The Maldives is generally considered a safe, relaxed destination. On local islands, respecting the dress code (shoulders and knees covered away from the beach) goes a long way. Choose accommodation with good reviews, book licensed excursion operators and share your plans with someone at home. Travelling in a small group makes everything easier, from boat transfers to evening walks.

How To Do An Affordable Maldives Trip

  • Base yourself on one local island near the activities you want (Dhigurah for whale sharks).
  • Use public or shared speedboats rather than seaplanes.
  • Choose half board so most meals are covered.
  • Do one resort day instead of a full resort stay.
  • Travel with a group to split boat and excursion costs, or join an all-planned group trip.

Visa, Flights & Practical Tips

Most nationalities get a free 30-day visa on arrival. You'll fly into Velana International Airport (MLE) in Malé. The Maldives is warm and beach-ready year round (around 28-31°C), with wetter months bringing fewer crowds and still plenty of sunshine. Don't bring alcohol through the airport, as it's not permitted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Maldives good for solo female travellers?

Yes. Local islands like Dhigurah make the Maldives affordable for solo travellers, and joining a women-only group trip makes it easy and social.

How can I do the Maldives on a budget?

Stay on a local island instead of a resort island, use speedboats rather than seaplanes, choose half board and do a resort day pass for the luxury experience.

Can you drink alcohol on local islands in the Maldives?

No. Alcohol isn't sold or permitted on local islands. Resorts and resort day passes are the exception. Our Maldives retreat's luxury resort day includes cocktails and mocktails.

What should I wear on a Maldives local island?

Cover shoulders and knees in the village and public areas. Bikinis and swimwear are fine on the designated bikini beach and on boat excursions.

Do I need a visa for the Maldives?

Most nationalities receive a free 30-day visa on arrival, so you don't need to apply in advance.
