I started That Girl Experiences because I wanted the kind of girls trip I always wished existed: for ocean lovers & adventurers, with a strong focus on friendships & community, lifemaxxing together, wellness & growth - one that's genuinely transformative. I've always had a strong vision of helping people become the best version of themselves, living life to the fullest and building a supportive community of women who build each other up & want to see each other succeed. I genuinely believe that my true, core purpose is to build a supportive female travel community to help women see the world, become the best version of themselves and live our lives to the fullest together.
